Low Motion is a free accessibility utility provided as-is.

Effective August 28, 2026

Purpose

The extension adjusts website animation and autoplay behavior for personal comfort. It is not a medical device, treatment, diagnostic tool, or anti-seizure certification, and it cannot guarantee that every source of motion will be removed.

Your control

Every profile is reversible. You can disable a site, choose another profile, allow a temporary exception, or uninstall the extension at any time.

Site compatibility

Websites implement motion in many ways. The extension respects browser security boundaries and may not affect protected browser pages, cross-origin media, canvas animation, or video that you start yourself. A website may also behave differently when motion is reduced.
